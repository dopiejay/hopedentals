# Hope Dental Surgery — Reconnaissance Script

Goal: in one visit or call, find out whether Hope is worth pursuing, and capture
the facts we currently don't have. Do **not** pitch the site details yet.

## Opening (do not lead with "you have no website")

> "I was researching Hope Dental Surgery and noticed most of the information I
> could find online comes through directories. I wanted to understand how you
> currently manage your online presence and whether you've considered having a
> dedicated website."

## Outcome decision tree (A–E)

- **A. No website, interested** → high-priority lead. Schedule a follow-up meeting.
- **B. Website planned** → offer to share the concept; find out who's building it.
- **C. Someone is developing one** → ask who/what stack; note and follow up later.
- **D. Not interested** → thank them, leave contact, move on.
- **E. Has a website but wants improvements** → audit what exists and reposition.

## Positioning note

Do not pitch "let me help a new clinic get started." Pitch:

> "You already have an established practice. Let's make the information patients
> find online reflect that practice properly."

## Exit

- If interested → offer a short branded concept (already built locally) tailored
  to whatever they confirm; agree next meeting.
- Always collect: primary phone, email, owner/manager name.
